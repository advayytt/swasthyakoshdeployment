Running without a language model

The interview, triage, coding, drug screening, FHIR export and PDF all work exactly as they do with a key. Two things change: case sheets are assembled by a deterministic template rather than written as prose, and uploaded documents arrive with empty fields for manual entry instead of being read automatically. Add GEMINI_API_KEY to .env and restart to turn both on.
